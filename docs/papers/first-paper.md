## Synopsis
This review explains how increasing ambient pressure affects lung volume, chest wall compliance, and blood shift in breath-hold divers. It provides a physiological framework that helps explain both adaptation and risk mechanisms such as lung squeeze.



## Abstract
This paper reviews the mechanical behavior of the respiratory system during breath-hold diving, with emphasis on pressure-related effects at depth.
