## The Question

Why can multiple “easy” dives become dangerous over time?

## Short Answer

Because oxygen debt accumulates while perceived effort remains low.

## Deeper Explanation

Discuss:
- incomplete recovery
- altered chemoreceptor response
- fatigue masking hypoxia

## What This Means for Freedivers

- Rest intervals matter more than depth
- Group diving increases hidden risk
- “Feeling fine” is misleading
